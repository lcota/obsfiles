# First 90 Days - Stakeholder Discovery

## Objectives 
- Understanding trading business across regions
- Identify technology opportunities 
- Build stakeholder network
- Create organizational Map

## Questions for Traders
- How the desk makes money  
    
- What is constraining performance  
    
- What matters most to the desk head  
    
- Where technology, data, and process can create value  
    
- How your organization is perceived  
    


  

My Recommended "Top 7"

If your calendar is packed and you only have 20-25 minutes, ask only these:

1. How does the desk make money?  
    
2. What are your top priorities this year?  
    
3. What are the biggest constraints on performance?  
    
4. If you could remove three problems tomorrow, what would they be?  
    
5. Where do traders lose the most time today?  
    
6. How can our organization better support your objectives?  
    
7. What would make you consider my first year a success?  
    

Those seven questions will likely generate 80% of the value you'll get from a first-round stakeholder interview during your SocGen onboarding. They are concise enough that executives will engage, yet broad enough to surface business strategy, pain points, politics, and opportunity areas simultaneously.
